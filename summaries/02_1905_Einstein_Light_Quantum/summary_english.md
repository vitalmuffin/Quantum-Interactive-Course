### 6. *About a

- According to Maxwell's theory, in all purely electromagnetic phenomena, including light, energy is to be understood as a continuous function of space, while the energy of a ponderable body is to be represented as a sum extended over the atoms and electrons according to the current view of physicists.
- The undulation theory of light, which operates with continuous spatial functions, has proven itself admirably for the representation of purely optical phenomena and will probably never be replaced by another theory.
- It is quite conceivable that the theory of light, which operates with continuous space functions, leads to contradictions with experience when applied to the phenomena of light production and light transformation.
- According to the assumption to be considered here, when a ray of light proceeds from a point propagates, the energy is not continuously distributed over spaces that become larger and larger, but consists of a finite number of quanta of energy localized in points of space, which move without dividing and can only be absorbed and produced as a whole.
