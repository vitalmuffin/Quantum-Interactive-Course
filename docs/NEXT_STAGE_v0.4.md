# Next stage — v0.4

## Purpose

Turn the historical core from 1900 to 1928 into a connected sequence of fully
interactive experiments rather than isolated paper cards.

## Planned laboratories

1. Planck radiation and the ultraviolet catastrophe
2. Einstein photoelectric effect
3. Bohr energy levels and spectral transitions
4. Compton scattering with energy and momentum conservation
5. de Broglie wavelength and the quantum-to-classical scale transition
6. double-slit experiment with mass, speed, geometry and decoherence
7. matrix mechanics and non-commuting operations
8. Schrödinger eigenstates and time evolution
9. Born probability and repeated measurements
10. uncertainty through Fourier-linked wave packets
11. Stern–Gerlach preparation and sequential measurements
12. Bose, Fermi and classical occupation statistics
13. Heitler–London bonding and Born–Oppenheimer separation
14. Dirac energy branches and the relativistic electron

## Cross-cutting requirements

- German and English from the start
- Temml equations with underbraced variables
- historical presets using the paper's own parameter regime where available
- a modern preset for comparison
- explicit distinction between prediction, observation and interpretation
- links to the relevant summary, image and PDF
- no unsupported quotations

## Definition of done

A learner can move chronologically from Planck to Dirac and, at every step,
see the anomaly, manipulate the proposed model, generate its prediction and
understand what previous assumption was restricted or replaced.
